The 9th Dragon

A brutal, tactical beat-’em-up set in Kowloon Walled City: hand-to-hand combat, firearms, stamina management and environmental finishers. I owned the combat pillar end to end through final production.

StudioFrameOver (freelance)
PublisherHeadup Games
EngineUnreal Engine 5
Time on projectMay – Sep 2026
Full breakdown → Watch on YouTube View on Steam My code

What I worked on

  • Rebuilt the core combat loop: introduced parry and counter mechanics, dynamic finishers driven by Motion Warping and stamina management, reworked the overall flow of combat, and added spatial detection queries so enemies position themselves smarter around the player.
  • Data-driven ranged combat: any enemy can use any weapon from data tables, switching between melee and ranged through a proxy Behavior Tree.
  • Modular enemy base and Behavior Tree architecture, with a moveset for every enemy type.
  • Game feel: hit-stop, camera shake scaled to move strength, finisher slow-motion, variable knockback and chain damage.
  • Tuned time-to-kill per enemy and per level for a progressive difficulty curve.

About Me

Lifelong gamer turned creator. I want to make games that give players the same immersion and joy that shaped who I am today.

Programmer at heart. I write gameplay code in C++ and C#: Unreal’s Gameplay Ability System, Behavior Trees and AI Perception, Unity state machines and custom editor tools. I care about modular, data-driven architecture that designers can drive without code, and about performance, from object pooling to profiling.

On the design side, I specialise in combat: the loop itself, the enemy AI behind it and the feedback layer that makes it read. I’m comfortable working to publisher milestones, running combat QA and owning a backlog.

I also review almost every game I play, breaking them down technically and from a player-experience point of view. Read my reviews here.

My top five games: Fallout: New Vegas, The Last of Us, Dark Souls III, Persona 5 and The Witcher 3.

Spanish (native) and English (C1).
